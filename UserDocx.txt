
Genarate The Important Files From STM32CubeMX based on your target MCU

Fill the stm32_toolchain_conf.cmake file to identify two important parameters

1. <TARGET_MCU> To specify target microcontroller Unit Wit it's peripherals
2. <MCU_FAMILY> The family to which MCU Belongs

Steps
--->Open up stm32_toolchain_conf.cmake
---> Change the Default value in file 
         set(TARGET_MCU <option>) write your option
         like STM32H750xx, line become set(TARGET_MCU STM32H750xx)

         set(MCU_FAMILY <option>) write your option
         like stm32h7xx, line become set(MCU_FAMILY stm32h7xx)
